# Severity vs. Priority

**Severity** is how much damage a bug does. **Priority** is how soon it should be fixed. A bug can be low severity and high priority, or the reverse.

If your team or client has its own definitions, use theirs.

## Severity

| Level | Impact |
|---|---|
| Critical | Data loss, a serious security problem, or the whole site or system is down |
| High | A major feature does not work, with no workaround |
| Medium | A feature partly works, or a workaround exists |
| Low | A cosmetic issue or minor inconvenience |

## Priority

| Level | Urgency |
|---|---|
| P1 | Fix now |
| P2 | Fix before the next release |
| P3 | Fix in normal planned work |
| P4 | Fix when time allows |

## Examples

- **Typo on the homepage:** Low severity. High priority the day before a launch.
- **Broken internal report:** High severity. Priority depends on who uses it, deadlines, and workarounds.
- **Slightly misaligned icon:** Low severity and low priority.
